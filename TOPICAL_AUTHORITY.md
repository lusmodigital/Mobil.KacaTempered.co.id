# Topical Authority — mobil.kacatempered.co.id

## Role and boundary

Mobil.KacaTempered.co.id is a specialist automotive-glass sales, installation, repair, and replacement property. The knowledge plan helps vehicle owners, fleets, workshops, and claims handlers identify glazing, assess damage, select the correct part, understand installation controls, manage cameras and sensors, and verify safe handover. Existing brand, type, model, and service routes own availability, quotations, location coverage, and conversion; articles provide neutral decision support and must not become model- or city-swapped sales pages.

Automotive glazing is safety-sensitive. A claim about tempered or laminated construction, optical quality, break behavior, marking, homologation, fitment, or feature compatibility requires the exact part marking plus current supplier or vehicle-manufacturer documentation and, where relevant, a test/certification record. Repair limits require a recognized repair procedure and qualified inspection. Bonding instructions, primers, cure conditions, and safe-drive-away time require the adhesive manufacturer's current TDS/SDS and recorded site conditions. ADAS, camera, sensor, HUD, antenna, heater, and rain-sensor work requires vehicle-specific service information, part data, diagnostic evidence, and any prescribed calibration report. Regulatory claims require current official Indonesian sources; insurance and warranty claims require the actual policy or written terms.

## Internal-link rule

Each KMO topic becomes a hub linking to its six briefs, and every brief links back to that hub. Selection flows from KMO-01 to construction, provenance, fitment, glass-position, and feature hubs. Damage pages link to immediate safety, then to the repair or replacement owner. Replacement articles link through bonding, workmanship handover, ADAS verification, documentation, and warranty. Repeated entities keep one intent owner: KMO-02 owns material construction, KMO-03 provenance, KMO-04 fitment, KMO-08 repair, KMO-09 bonding, KMO-11 electronics/calibration, KMO-14 defects, and KMO-15 regulation. Commercial routes receive links only after the reader has reached a documented part or service decision.
