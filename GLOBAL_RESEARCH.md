### KR-02 — Current Indonesian mandatory SNI regime for motor-vehicle safety glass

- **Sources:** [Permenperin No. 15 Tahun 2025 — official BPK record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/321157/permenperin-no-15-tahun-2025); [official regulation PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/396468/permenperin-no-15-tahun-2025.pdf); [SNI 8210:2018 — official BSN record](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/11826-sni82102018).
- **Grade:** 1.
- **Purpose:** Establish the current Indonesian product-compliance baseline without importing building-glass rules.
- **Summary:** Permenperin No. 15/2025 makes the Indonesian National Standard for motor-vehicle safety glass mandatory and replaced the 2015 regime. BSN lists SNI 8210:2018 as current for laminated and tempered safety glazing used on motor vehicles and trailers.
- **Grounded facts:** The regulation was set on 27 March 2025, promulgated on 25 April 2025, and became effective on 25 October 2025. The public SNI scope covers safety glazing for vehicle categories L, M, N, O, and T; excludes bullet-resistant and double glazing; and states that tempered windshield use is limited to vehicles whose speed is below 40 km/h.
- **Incorporation:** Governs Indonesian compliance framing in `KMO-02`, `KMO-03`, `KMO-05`, `KMO-06`, and `KMO-15`. Describe the SNI scope and current mandatory regime, then require product-specific conformity evidence.
- **Limits/recheck:** The public BSN page is an abstract, not the full 81-page standard. Exact classifications, markings, specimens, test methods, acceptance criteria, certification routes, exemptions, transition provisions, and surveillance require the licensed standard plus the complete current regulation and implementation documents (`GATE-01`, `GATE-04`).

### KR-08 — Solar, luminous, color, heated, and switchable glazing are separate performance domains

- **Sources:** [ISO 13837:2021 solar/luminous/color measurements](https://www.iso.org/standard/76159.html); [ISO 17449:2015 electrically heated glazing](https://www.iso.org/standard/59799.html); [ISO 11983:2025 electro-switchable glazing](https://www.iso.org/standard/83980.html).
- **Grade:** 2.
- **Purpose:** Keep specialty-feature metrics distinct and tied to named test evidence.
- **Summary:** ISO 13837 covers luminous and solar transmittance and colorimetry for road-vehicle glazing; ISO 17449 covers static performance of electrically heated glazing; ISO 11983 covers electro-switchable glazing characteristics.
- **Grounded facts:** VLT, UV behavior, solar-energy behavior, color, heater performance, haze, and switching behavior are not interchangeable metrics. ISO 17449's abstract cautions that specimen tests do not necessarily represent in-vehicle performance.
- **Incorporation:** Supports `KMO-02-04`, `KMO-04-03`, `KMO-05-04`–`KMO-05-05`, `KMO-06-03`, `KMO-11-05`, and `KMO-12-01`–`KMO-12-03`. Require the named metric, method, and whole glazing/film stack.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not infer cabin comfort, legal compliance, camera compatibility, heater function, or field durability from one laboratory metric. Obtain the exact part/product report and installed-system evidence (`GATE-03`, `GATE-04`, `GATE-11`).

### KR-09 — Tempered, laminated, and specialty construction boundaries

- **Sources:** [Pilkington Automotive definitions](https://www.pilkington.com/en/us/automotive/products/definitions); [Pilkington automotive processes](https://my.pilkington.com/sitecore/content/pilkington/uk/automotive/original-equipment/automotive-glazing/automotive-processes?sc_lang=en-GB); [AGC laminated acoustic glass](https://www.agc-automotive.com/en/products-and-solutions/laminated-acoustic-glass).
- **Grade:** 4.
- **Purpose:** Provide clear manufacturer-grounded construction explanations while preserving vehicle- and part-specific exceptions.
- **Summary:** Laminated glazing bonds glass plies with an interlayer that retains fragments; tempered glazing is heat-treated and designed to break into small blunt pieces. Side, rear, and roof positions may use tempered or laminated constructions, and acoustic/solar/security features can be incorporated into a laminate.
- **Grounded facts:** Manufacturer sources describe PVB-style laminated construction and heat/cool tempering processes. Pilkington notes laminated glazing is increasingly used in side windows; AGC identifies laminated acoustic glazing across windshield, side, rear, and roof positions.
- **Incorporation:** Core grounding for `KMO-02`, plus position decisions in `KMO-05` and `KMO-06`. Tell readers to verify the actual marking and documentation rather than assume construction from position alone.
- **Limits/recheck:** Manufacturer education pages do not approve a particular part, emergency-egress plan, or Indonesian application. Do not generalize brand performance claims, exact thicknesses, penetration times, or universal position rules. Resolve actual part construction (`GATE-03`, `GATE-04`).

### KR-17 — Leak, noise, distortion, and mechanism complaints need diagnosis before remedy

- **Sources:** [ANSI/AGSC/AGRSS 005-2022 public standard](https://agsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ANSIAGSCAGRSS005-2022.pdf); [ISO 3538:1997 optical tests](https://www.iso.org/standard/8928.html); [Honda 2025 Civic Hatchback front-camera owner guidance](https://techinfo.honda.com/rjanisis/pubs/OM/AH/AT402525IOM/enu/details/131271047-84907.html).
- **Grade:** 2 for optical test scope; 3 for replacement assessment; 4 for named-vehicle optical-interface risk.
- **Purpose:** Provide a safe diagnostic structure for installed-glass complaints.
- **Summary:** Similar symptoms can originate in different systems: leaks from glazing retention, body seams, drains, roof modules, or condensation; noise from alignment, trim, openings, or body conditions; visual complaints from glass optics, seating/view angle, contamination, film, or camera/HUD interfaces; moving-window complaints from glass, guides, regulator, fasteners, or anti-pinch setup.
- **Grounded facts:** AGRSS makes vehicle condition and restoration part of replacement assessment. ISO 3538 establishes that automotive optical properties have defined test methods. Honda demonstrates that even localized glass conditions can matter to an optical sensor.

### KR-18 — Insurance, warranty, and lifecycle outcomes depend on actual written terms

- **Sources:** [AAUI standard-policy library](https://aaui.or.id/polis/); [Polis Standar Asuransi Kendaraan Bermotor Indonesia](https://aaui.or.id/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/11-PSAKBI-rev-dispute-2021.pdf); [ANSI/AGSC/AGRSS 005-2022 public standard](https://agsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ANSIAGSCAGRSS005-2022.pdf).
- **Grade:** 3 for the industry policy wording and replacement record framework; 5 for any actual claim/warranty outcome.
- **Purpose:** Ground cost/claim/warranty articles without promising coverage, approval, part grade, reimbursement, or remedy.
- **Summary:** The AAUI standard policy makes coverage depend on the policy, schedule, clauses, endorsements, covered event, exclusions, duties, evidence, and claim handling. Replacement records can help document identity, materials, work, restrictions, and follow-on issues, but do not themselves create coverage or warranty rights.
- **Grounded facts:** Standard wording is a baseline document, while the insured's issued policy and endorsements control the actual contract. Glass-only coverage, deductible/own risk, preferred workshop, part type, film, calibration, tax, towing, downtime, and consequential damage must be confirmed in the actual claim.
- **Incorporation:** Governs all `KMO-16`, with evidence-capture links from `KMO-07-05`, `KMO-10-06`, `KMO-11-03`, and `KMO-15-05`–`KMO-15-06`.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not promise acceptance, cashless service, genuine/OE parts, calibration reimbursement, warranty duration, or a cause-of-loss decision. Obtain the issued policy/schedule/endorsements, insurer authorization, estimate/invoice, evidence, and written warranty (`GATE-10`).

## Jurisdiction, access, and professional-use rules

- Indonesian statutes, regulations, official BSN records, and their current implementation control Indonesian legal/compliance statements. UN, ISO, AGRSS, ROLAGS, OEM, and manufacturer sources are technical evidence within their stated scope—not automatic Indonesian law.
- Public abstracts establish scope only. Licensed/full standards, paid OEM service information, certificates, test reports, issued policies, TDS/SDS, and calibration reports must be obtained when a brief crosses the relevant gate.
- Automotive glazing is the only standards domain in scope. Do not use architectural/building-glass SNI, façade, building-film, or building-installation rules as evidence for motor vehicles.
- Consumer articles may explain observable checks, documentation, and escalation. Cutting/removal, bonding, corrosion work, invasive trim/roof/mechanism work, electrical intervention, resin repair, and ADAS calibration remain professional tasks.
- “Safe,” “compliant,” “repairable,” “calibrated,” “covered,” and “warranted” are outcomes requiring the applicable evidence gate; they are not generic product adjectives.
